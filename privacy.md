# CasePilot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

## 1. Overview

CasePilot is designed to help users organize life-admin cases, deadlines, documents, reminders, follow-ups, and related tasks.

## 2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, CasePilot may process information such as:

- Case titles and task details
- Deadlines and reminders
- Notes and follow-up information
- Documents, images, and extracted text
- Information you voluntarily provide to AI features

## 3. AI Processing

CasePilot may send information needed to provide AI features to third-party AI service providers.

AI processing is used to provide features such as document understanding, planning, drafting, and conversational assistance.

## 4. Documents

If you upload a document or image, CasePilot may process it to provide features such as text extraction and document insights.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to upload and process the information you provide.

## 5. Subscriptions and Payments

CasePilot may use RevenueCat and applicable app-store payment systems to manage subscriptions and purchases.

Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider rather than stored directly by CasePilot.

## 6. Notifications

CasePilot may schedule local notifications for deadlines, reminders, and follow-ups when you enable or use these features.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by CasePilot. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

CasePilot may store information locally on your device or through services required to provide specific features.

You may delete information from the app where deletion functionality is provided.

## 9. Third-Party Services

CasePilot may use third-party services for functionality such as AI processing, analytics, subscription management, hosting, and other technical services.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 10. Children's Privacy

CasePilot is not specifically directed toward children. Users should comply with applicable age requirements for the services they use.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## 12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the CasePilot team through the contact information provided with the application.
